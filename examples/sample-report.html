Executive summary

Contoso Travel Desk is a customer-facing Copilot Studio agent operated by Contoso Travel Ltd (UK-established). It answers booking questions, retrieves reservation status from Dataverse, and raises amendment requests. Customers in Ireland and France are intentionally served, so the EU AI Act Article 2 and EU GDPR Article 3(2)(a) territorial tests are both met alongside UK law.

The agent is not high-risk under EU AI Act Article 6 / Annex III — it does not make or materially influence any listed decision. Article 50(1) interaction transparency applies from 2 August 2026 and is now in force.

One High finding blocks safe go-live: a Dataverse amendment action runs under a maker connection with broad read access while the conversation itself is anonymous. Two Medium findings concern the now-in-force AI disclosure duty and persisted booking data with no observable minimisation control. Six items await tenant or organisational evidence and are not severity-rated.

Counts: 1 High, 2 Medium, 1 Low, 2 Advisory / future readiness, 6 Evidence required.

Findings

CTD-01 — Privileged Dataverse action reachable from anonymous conversation

High
Basis typeTechnical control, with UK/EU GDPR Art. 32 exposure (Art. 32)
ApplicabilityPersonal data (reservations) is processed; security must be appropriate to risk. Anonymous public channel + maker-credential write action creates an authorization gap.
Effective statusIn force
Evidenceactions/AmendBooking.mcs.yml:12 uses connection contoso_dataverse_maker; settings.mcs.yml:8 sets authentication to none; channel screenshot confirms public website deployment.
RemediationRequire end-user authentication for the amendment path, or replace the maker connection with a least-privilege service identity scoped to the caller's own booking with server-side verification.
OwnerSecurity / Tenant administrator

CTD-02 — No AI-interaction disclosure (Article 50(1) now in force)

Medium
Basis typeBinding law — EU AI Act Art. 50(1) (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)
ApplicabilityEU users are intentionally served; duty applies from 2 August 2026. The branded "Travel Desk" persona does not make the AI nature obvious to a reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect person.
Effective statusIn force since 2 August 2026
EvidenceSearched topics/Greeting.mcs.yaml, agent.mcs.yaml instructions, and all welcome/fallback topics for disclosure language; no match found. Host-page framing not yet supplied (see EV-2) — rated Medium rather than High pending that context.
RemediationAdd a clear first-turn disclosure to the greeting topic (e.g. "I'm Contoso's AI travel assistant") and mirror it in the host-page introduction.
OwnerYAML / Operations

CTD-03 — Booking and contact details persisted with no observable minimisation or retention control

Medium
Basis typeBinding law — UK/EU GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) and 5(1)(e) (Art. 5)
ApplicabilityThe agent collects email, booking reference, and free-text amendment details and writes them to Dataverse; minimisation and storage limitation apply to each field and to transcripts.
Effective statusIn force
Evidencetopics/AmendBooking.mcs.yaml:24-41 collects five fields where two are used by the action; workflows/LogRequest/workflow.json writes full conversation text to a custom table. No retention configuration is observable in source (transcript settings pending, EV-1).
RemediationDrop the unused fields, store only the amendment payload rather than full conversation text, and define a retention period for the custom table aligned to the booking lifecycle.
OwnerYAML / Privacy

CTD-04 — Fallback topic reveals internal error detail

Low
Basis typeTechnical control (UK principle: safety, security and robustness — regulatory guidance)
ApplicabilityGood-practice robustness; no binding provision engaged on these facts.
Effective statusGuidance
Evidencetopics/Fallback.mcs.yaml:18 interpolates the raw connector error message into the user reply.
RemediationReplace with a generic apology and log the detail server-side.
OwnerYAML

Remediation plan

Tenant / administrator actions

1. Require authentication or re-scope the amendment action's connection (CTD-01 — go-live blocker). 2. Supply transcript retention settings and DLP policies (EV-1, EV-3). 3. Configure retention for the custom request table (CTD-03).

Organisational actions
